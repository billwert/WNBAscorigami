FAQ

What is Scorigami?

Scorigami is a concept thought up by Jon Bois. A Scorigami is a final score that has never happend before. For more info, check out this video made by Jon Bois about Scorigami.

How do I read this chart?

The winning team's score is along the left (vertical axis) and the losing team's score is along the bottom (horizontal axis). Scores marked in orange have happened, scores in white have not yet happened, scores in grey are impossible. You can hover on a cell to get more info.
