Frequently Asked Questions

What is Scorigami?

Scorigami is a concept thought up by Jon Bois. A Scorigami is a final score that has never happend before. For more info, check out the video made by Jon Bois about Scorigami below.

How do I read this chart?

The winning team's score is along the top (horizontal axis) and the losing team's score is along the right (vertical axis). Scores marked in orange have happened, scores in white have not yet happened, scores in grey are impossible. You can click a cell to get more info.
